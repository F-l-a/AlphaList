Notifications

▸ Web Method (PC):

  1. Visit https://ntfy.pokemmotools.org/alphapings
  2. Allow notifications when your browser prompts you (from the left menu)
  3. Keep the browser and the tab open in the background

▸ Mobile App (iOS/Android):

  1. Download ntfy from App Store or Google Play
  2. Open the app and enable notifications (disable the battery optimization if prompted).
  3. Subscribe to topic: alphapings (all lowercase)
  4. Tap on "Use another server" and input: https://ntfy.pokemmotools.org
You will get a notification from the Browser or the ntfy App when an Alpha is reported by a trusted player. Check your notification/battery saving settings if you don't receive it.
Check dontkillmyapp.com if your Android is killing the ntfy app.
To send notifications as a trusted player, DM @lorddusk to get your credentials. You don't need credentials to receive the notifications.
